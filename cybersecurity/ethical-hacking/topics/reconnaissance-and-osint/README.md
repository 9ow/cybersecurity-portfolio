# Reconnaissance and open-source intelligence

Reconnaissance establishes a reliable picture of publicly visible infrastructure and context while controlling collection scope, source freshness and information disclosure.

[Detailed OSINT and footprinting reference: search operators, WHOIS, DNS, ping, traceroute, TTL/MTU, Netcraft, theHarvester, WhatWeb, WAFW00F, HTTrack and social engineering considerations](osint-techniques-and-tooling.md).

**Core distinction:** indexed information and inferred technology identities are leads; they do not prove the presence of a weakness or grant permission for intrusive validation.
