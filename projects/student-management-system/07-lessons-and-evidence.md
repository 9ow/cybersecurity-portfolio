# Lessons Learned and Evidence Status

## Second-year work completed
The project report documents a complete set of academic **systems analysis and design deliverables**, including a service request, task schedule, Ishikawa diagram, requirements traceability matrix, role and use-case glossary, five detailed use-case narratives, use-case diagram, report-generation activity diagram, report-generation sequence diagram, multiple interface mockups, stakeholder interview and feasibility/cost-benefit analysis.

## Skills practiced
- Turning stakeholder needs into testable functional and non-functional requirements.
- Recognizing actors, roles, preconditions, triggers, successful and exceptional workflows.
- Distinguishing a **use-case model** (actor goals), **activity diagram** (process/decisions), **sequence diagram** (participants/messages ordered by time) and **class diagram** (static domain structure).
- Designing programme applications, course enrolment, attendance, authentication and report generation.
- Checking consistency among workflows, business rules, requirements and UI mockups.
- Preparing a Gantt schedule, estimating technical/economic feasibility, and interpreting NPV/payback analysis.
