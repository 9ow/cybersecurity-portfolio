# Project Planning and Feasibility

The second-year report documents a Microsoft Project schedule and Gantt chart covering initiation, problem analysis, interviews, requirements, use-case/behavior modeling, technical and economic feasibility, user interface design, planned testing and documentation.

## What I learned
- **Task dependencies:** some outputs depend on earlier tasks, such as finalized requirements before detailed UI design.
- **Milestones:** schedule reviews and final report submissions give the team explicit checkpoints.
- **Ishikawa analysis:** group possible causes of inefficient student administration into people, processes, training, technology and environment.
- **Technical feasibility:** determine whether the proposed software can work with expected infrastructure and integrations.
- **Operational feasibility:** determine whether staff can use and support the system.
- **Economic feasibility:** estimate cost, benefits and recurring operational expenditure.
- **NPV:** discount expected future cash flows to their present value.
- **Payback period:** estimate when accumulated benefits recover initial investment.

The schedule and feasibility charts document *analysis and proposals*, not proof of a live deployment or realized profit. No participant names, personal IDs or source-document signatures are published.
